# Data Provenance

## Controlling source classes
- National Board of Revenue: tax law, schedules, forms and notices
- Directorate of Secondary and Higher Education: School/College MPO policy, EFT and allowance notices
- Future packages use their own controlling authorities

## Evidence rule
A rule may be marked `Verified` only when:
- the authority and document are identified;
- the extracted rule is recorded;
- the effective date is recorded;
- a source ID is linked inside the workbook.

Community posts, videos and social-media discussions may inform user-research questions, but they do not control formulas.
