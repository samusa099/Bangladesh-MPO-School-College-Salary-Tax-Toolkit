# Scope Boundaries

The current repository is limited to the MPO School and College salary-tax toolkit.

## Included now

- MPO School salary planning
- MPO College salary planning
- Annual salary statement
- Tax Year 2026-27 estimate
- Taxpayer category comparison
- Payslip view
- Income-expense dashboard
- Validation cases

## Not included in this repository

These should be separate future packages:

- Madrasa MPO toolkit
- Independent Ebtedayi toolkit
- Technical and Vocational MPO toolkit
- Government and autonomous salary toolkit
- Private and NGO salary toolkit
- Individual tax and personal finance toolkit
- Small business toolkit
- VAT and TDS toolkit
- Import-export and LC toolkit
- Company tax toolkit

## Reason

Each area uses different rules, authorities and validation needs. Mixing them in one workbook would make the project harder to audit and maintain.
