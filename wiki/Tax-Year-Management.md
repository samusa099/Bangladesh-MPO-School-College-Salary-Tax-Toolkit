# Tax Year Management

The project should keep each tax year separate.

## Rule

Do not overwrite older tax-year workbooks.

## Recommended file naming

```text
MPO_School_College_Salary_Tax_BD_TY2026_27.xlsx
MPO_School_College_Salary_Tax_BD_TY2027_28.xlsx
```

## Why this matters

- tax slabs may change;
- thresholds may change;
- allowances may change;
- policy effective dates may differ;
- users may need historical records.

## Release practice

Each tax year should have its own workbook, release note and validation status.
