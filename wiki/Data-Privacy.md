# Data Privacy

## Public data rule

Do not publish real employee personal data.

## Do not commit

- NID information;
- bank account numbers;
- EFT credentials;
- tax portal credentials;
- real payroll statements with identifiable employees;
- medical or family-sensitive information;
- temporary files containing personal information.

## Acceptable examples

- synthetic employee rows;
- anonymized salary scenarios;
- formula-only validation cases;
- public source references;
- screenshots with no personal data.

## Workbook public release rule

Before public release, check every sheet for hidden personal data, comments, metadata and external links.
