# Limitations

## Current limitations

- The workbook is still Beta.
- Some allowance and deduction values remain editable.
- Official salary-row reconciliation is not complete yet.
- Practitioner review is pending.
- Only School and College MPO scope is included.

## Not covered yet

- Madrasa MPO rules
- Ebtedayi rules
- Technical and Vocational MPO rules
- Government salary rules
- Private salary rules
- Full individual tax filing workflow
- Company tax
- VAT and TDS
- Import-export and LC

## User expectation

Use the workbook for structured planning and review, then verify final figures with current official sources and appropriate reviewers.
