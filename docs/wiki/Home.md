# Bangladesh MPO School & College Salary Tax Toolkit Wiki

Welcome to the project wiki for the **Bangladesh MPO School & College Salary Tax Toolkit**.

This project provides an open-source Excel-based toolkit for MPO-listed School & College employees in Bangladesh to prepare salary statements, tax estimates, payslips and financial dashboards.
