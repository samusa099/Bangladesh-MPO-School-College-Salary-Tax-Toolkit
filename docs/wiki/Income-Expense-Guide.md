# Income Expense Guide

The `INCOME_EXPENSE` sheet helps users compare annual net salary with spending and savings.

## Common categories

- household contribution;
- food and groceries;
- housing;
- utilities;
- medical;
- education;
- transportation;
- debt or loan;
- personal spending;
- family support;
- savings;
- investment;
- tax payment;
- other.

## Purpose

This sheet helps users understand salary allocation and annual surplus or deficit.
