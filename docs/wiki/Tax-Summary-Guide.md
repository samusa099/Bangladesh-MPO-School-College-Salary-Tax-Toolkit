# Tax Summary Guide

The `TAX_SUMMARY` sheet gives a compact working summary.

## Typical fields

- taxpayer name;
- tax year;
- gross salary;
- verified salary exemption;
- other taxable income;
- taxable income;
- applicable threshold;
- estimated tax liability;
- TDS deducted;
- balance payable or excess TDS.

## Purpose

This sheet is designed as a working summary for review and preparation, not as a replacement for official forms.
