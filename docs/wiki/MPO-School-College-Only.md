# MPO School and College Only

This repository is currently limited to MPO School and College salary-tax work.

## Why the boundary exists

School/College, Madrasa, Ebtedayi and Technical/Vocational institutions do not all use the same rule base. Mixing them into one workbook too early would reduce audit quality.
