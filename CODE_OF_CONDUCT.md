# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Bangladesh MPO School & College Salary Tax Toolkit community respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors should:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- focus reviews on evidence, formulas, sources, and project scope;
- protect personal, payroll, banking, tax-return, and institutional data;
- distinguish verified rules from assumptions and Beta placeholders;
- accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes transparently.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, disclosure of private data, and repeated disregard for project scope are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens contributors, users, data safety, or project integrity.

Report conduct concerns privately through the process described in `SECURITY.md` or through an appropriate private contact channel maintained by the repository owner.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, Wiki contributions, and other project-related spaces.

## Attribution

This policy is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant, adapted for this project’s payroll, tax, documentation, and data-governance context.
